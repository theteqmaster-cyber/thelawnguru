Comprehensive Outdoor Services

Everything Your Yard Needs To Thrive

We combine horticulturally sound methods with top quality materials to guarantee lasting beauty and rapid rooting.

1. Site Prep & Red Soil Conditioning

Harare's red clay and rocky subsoils need proper cultivation. We break hardpans, clear rubble, and grade the topography to prevent water ponding.

  • Thorough de-weeding & root extraction
  • Premium weed-free dark topsoil delivery
  • Precision laser and stringline grading

2. Instant Turf Supply & Laying

Freshly harvested sod delivered straight to your property and laid brick-pattern with seamless joints. Rollers are used to ensure direct root-soil contact.

  • Same-day harvesting to prevent shock
  • Professional roller compacting
  • High-phosphorus root boost fertilizer

3. Edging & Hardscape Borders

Crisp edges distinguish a mediocre lawn from an exquisite estate. We install concrete curbs, brick pavers, stone retaining contours, and decorative pebbles.

  • Clean separation between lawn & drive
  • Stepping stone walkways
  • Low maintenance weed barrier liners
Grass Varieties Catalog

Selecting The Right Grass for Your Harare Property

Harare's high altitude and sun patterns require carefully matching grass species to your yard's shade, foot traffic, and water availability.

Most Popular

Durban Grass

Vibrant emerald green with broad, soft blades. Exceptionally comfortable for barefoot walking and very fast to establish in Harare.

Sun / Shade: Sun & Semi-Shade
Water Needs: Moderate
Traffic Tolerance: High
Best Suited For: Family Homes, Kids, Pets
Heavy Traffic

Kikuyu Grass

A vigorous, dense turf that repairs itself rapidly from wear. Ideal for large yards, schools, sports fields, and full-sun properties.

Sun / Shade: Full Sun Only
Water Needs: Moderate to High
Traffic Tolerance: Very High
Best Suited For: Expansive Yards, Dogs
Drought Hardy

Buffalo Grass

Sturdy, deep-rooted grass that maintains color with lower water requirements. Outstanding performance on dry Harare slopes.

Sun / Shade: Full Sun to Part Shade
Water Needs: Low (Drought Resilient)
Traffic Tolerance: Moderate
Best Suited For: Water-conscious yards
Fine Velvet

Pasadena / Winter Green

Fine-textured ornamental grass offering a velvety putting-green appearance. Stays remarkably green through Harare's chilly dry winter months.

Sun / Shade: Full Sun
Water Needs: High
Traffic Tolerance: Moderate
Best Suited For: Luxury Front Show Lawns
